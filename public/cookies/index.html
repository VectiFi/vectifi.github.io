Cookie Policy

Effective 30 July 2026 · Last updated 30 July 2026

Short version: this website sets no cookies at all. There is no consent banner here because there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, this page changes with it — and we will ask permission first where the law requires it.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

A cookie is a small text file a website asks your browser to store, so that it can recognise your browser on a later visit. Related technologies do much the same job by other means: local and session storage keep data in your browser; pixels, tags and web beacons are tiny invisible resources whose loading tells a third party that you viewed a page; and fingerprinting infers a stable identity from your device’s characteristics.

They are commonly grouped as strictly necessary (the site cannot function without them), preferences, statistics/analytics, and marketing. Only the first group can normally be set without asking you first.

2. Which of these vectifi.com uses

None of them. Specifically, this website has:

CategoryUsed here?Detail
Strictly necessary cookiesNoneThe site is static and needs no session or login state
Preference cookiesNoneNo settings are stored between visits
Statistics / analyticsNoneNo analytics product is installed
Marketing / advertisingNoneNo ad, remarketing or conversion tags
Pixels, tags, web beaconsNone—
Local / session storageNoneBoth remain empty
Consent management platformNoneNot needed, as nothing above is set

This is not an aspiration — it is what the site actually does. We checked by loading the published site in a real browser and inspecting every cookie jar plus local and session storage after browsing the whole page. All were empty.

3. Third-party content

Embedded third-party content is the usual way tracking arrives on a site that has none of its own. We keep that surface deliberately small:

  • Fonts are self-hosted. We serve the Inter typeface from our own domain instead of a third-party font CDN, so viewing a page here does not disclose your IP address to a font provider.
  • No embedded video, maps, chat widgets or social buttons. These typically set cookies of their own the moment they load.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs requests (including IP addresses) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. That is server logging, not a cookie, and you cannot opt out of it without not visiting.

4. How to control cookies anyway

There is nothing here for you to block, but every browser lets you review and delete cookies for any site, and refuse them by default — look for “Cookies and site data” under Privacy settings in Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge. Blocking cookies entirely will not break this website, since it does not use any. Browsers also offer “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals; we have nothing to apply them to, because we do not track you and do not sell or share personal information.

5. What happens if this changes

If we add analytics or any other non-essential technology, we will update this page before or at the point it goes live, and — where the UK/EU ePrivacy rules or similar laws apply — ask for your consent before anything is set, with a genuine option to decline. We would also list what is set and why, rather than only linking to a vendor tool.

6. What this page does not cover

This policy covers the public website at vectifi.com only. The VectiFi application at my.vectifi.com is a signed-in product with different technical requirements, and is covered by the agreement you enter into when you receive an account.
