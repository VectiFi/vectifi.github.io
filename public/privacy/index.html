Privacy Policy

Effective 30 July 2026 · Last updated 30 July 2026

Short version: this website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or any other tracking technology. The only personal information we collect here is the work email address you choose to give us when you request beta access.

1. Who we are and what this covers

VectiFi, Inc. (“VectiFi”, “we”, “us”) provides an AI-native financial analytics platform. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our public website at vectifi.com.

This policy covers this website only. It does not cover the VectiFi application at my.vectifi.com, which handles customer financial data under the separate agreement you enter into when you receive an account.

You can reach us about anything in this policy at sysadmin@vectifi.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

The only form on this website is the beta access request. When you submit it, we collect the email address you enter. We require a company or organisation email address and reject common personal-mailbox domains, so the address you give us is normally a work address that identifies you in a professional capacity.

You may also contact us directly by email. In that case we receive whatever information you choose to put in your message.

2.2 Information collected automatically

This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives and logs technical information necessary to deliver the page — including your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, and your browser user-agent string. These logs are generated and controlled by our hosting provider for delivery, security and abuse prevention. We do not build profiles from them and we have no analytics layer on top of them.

2.3 Cookies and similar technologies

This website sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising or remarketing tags, no pixels or web beacons, no session or local storage, and no consent management platform. We verified this by auditing the site as served in production: no cookies are written in any storage partition, and browser local and session storage remain empty.

Because we set no cookies other than none at all, there is nothing for you to consent to and no cookie banner on this site. Fonts are served from our own domain rather than a third-party font CDN, so loading a page here does not disclose your IP address to a font provider.

If we later add analytics or any non-essential cookie, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before setting them. Our Cookie Policy breaks this down by category.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your beta access request and contact you about the beta programme.
  • To send you information about VectiFi that you have asked for.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the website.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use it for automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

4. Legal bases (UK/EU visitors)

Where the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases: consent, which you give by submitting your email address, for beta and marketing contact — you can withdraw it at any time; and our legitimate interests in operating and securing our website and in responding to enquiries about our product.

6. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers process information there. Where personal information is transferred from the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated into our providers’ data processing terms as the transfer safeguard.

7. How long we keep it

We keep beta access requests for up to 24 months after your last interaction with us, and then delete them — or sooner if you ask us to. We keep the information only for as long as we are still plausibly acting on your request to hear about the beta. Hosting logs are retained on our hosting provider’s own schedule.

8. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to delete your email address from our beta list at any time by emailing us at the address below — no account or verification hoops.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access the personal information we hold about you; to have it corrected or deleted; to object to or restrict how we use it; to receive a portable copy; and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that already happened. UK and EU residents may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. California residents have the rights to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing — we do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

9. Children

This website is a business product and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and beta submissions are transmitted encrypted in transit to our processor. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes — particularly the introduction of any tracking technology — will be brought to your attention on the website itself.

12. Contact us

Questions, or want your information deleted? Email sysadmin@vectifi.com and we will action it. We aim to respond to any privacy request within 30 days.
